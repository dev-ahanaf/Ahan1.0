BEFORE YOU START

3 simple steps

01

Connect your ESP32

Use a data-capable USB cable and connect the board to your computer.

02

Choose the serial port

Click Install, select your ESP32 serial port, then allow access.

03

Flash & configure

Install the firmware. Follow the OLED / serial instructions to configure Wi-Fi and pair to your own server.

💡 Not showing a port? Try another USB data cable, CH340/CP210x driver, or hold BOOT while connecting if your board requires it.

These are the GPIOs in your uploaded SKR wiring diagram. The published release metadata must confirm the XiaoZhi profile. LED: GPIO18 (function depends on firmware).

AFTER FLASHING

Your server. Your assistant.

①

Connect the device to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi using the firmware's supported provisioning flow.

②

Make sure the configured OTA / WebSocket server is reachable from the device.

③

Pair the ESP32 in your own Falcon AI console and assign an AI agent.

Wi-Fi provisioning and pairing must actually be implemented in the firmware and server; web flashing alone does not provide them.

Important: Flashing can erase existing firmware, Wi-Fi settings and saved data. Back up anything you need. The website checks the ESP32 chip family; you must still verify the pinout and installed flash size.
